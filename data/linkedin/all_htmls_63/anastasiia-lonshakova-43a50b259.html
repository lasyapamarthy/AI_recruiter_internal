Recently, I encountered an interesting bug at work. When a user tried to log in, he received an error message: "value.toString is not a function." At first, I thought, "Oh, this will be a piece of cake." Spoiler alert: it wasn’t. After reading the bug report, I discovered that the issue had been reproduced three times by three different users during December. However, neither I nor the testers could replicate it. There was no reference to "value.toString" in the codebase, and we didn’t have any additional information. So, I decided to turn this into a little investigation. I started by checking the code and realized that this error was being thrown in the .catch block after the Apollo Client login mutation. Since our PHP backend couldn’t be the source of this issue, I suspected it might be something related to Apollo Client. A quick Google search for this error in connection with Apollo Client led me to a thread on the Apollo GitHub page. Turns out, the issue was related to a Kaspersky product — users with Kaspersky installed on their machines were encountering this error. Kaspersky confirmed this and promised to fix it in a future release. In the meantime, Apollo recommended upgrading the Apollo Client to version 3.11.8 or later as a workaround. It’s always fascinating how sometimes the most unexpected sources can be the root cause of a bug!
