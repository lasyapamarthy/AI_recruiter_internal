Quality Assurance - QA Professional, Testing, Test Automation, SDETQuality Assurance - QA Professional, Testing, Test Automation, SDET Dr. Dhríti Tupe, GxP Expert ® • 3rd+Dr. Dhríti Tupe, GxP Expert ® • 3rd+ Quality Control (QC), Quality Assurance (QA), and Quality Control Management are distinct concepts aimed at ensuring the quality of products or services: - **Quality Control (QC)** involves inspecting, testing, and evaluating products or services to meet specific quality standards. It's reactive, addressing defects post-production or service delivery, with the goal of eliminating defective items before reaching customers. - **Quality Assurance (QA)** is proactive, encompassing activities to prevent defects and ensure products or services meet requirements. It focuses on creating systems, procedures, and processes for consistent quality, aiming to prevent defects rather than just detecting and correcting them. - **Quality Control Management** is the overall management and coordination of QC activities within an organization. It includes planning, organizing, and controlling resources and personnel to effectively implement QC procedures, ensuring they're integrated into the organization's management system. In essence, QC inspects and tests products, QA prevents defects proactively, and QC Management coordinates these activities within the organization.
