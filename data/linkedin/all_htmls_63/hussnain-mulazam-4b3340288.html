Examples include: Chatbots: AI agents that interact with users via text or voice. Software agents: Programs that monitor systems, automate tasks, or gather data (e.g., web crawlers). Intelligent agents: AI systems that perceive their environment and take actions to achieve goals, like self-driving car software. Chemical or Biological Agent: In science, an agent is a substance or organism that causes a specific effect or reaction. Examples: Chemical agent: A substance used in reactions, like a cleaning agent or nerve agent. Pathogenic agent: A microbe causing disease, like a virus or bacteria. Government or Espionage Agent: A person working for a government agency, often in intelligence or law enforcement, like a CIA or FBI agent.
