About

Highly motivated and results-oriented Front End Engineer with 6+ years of experience delivering high-performance, secure web applications within the financial industry. Proven ability to architect and implement complex solutions that meet stringent security and compliance standards.

Expertise lies in frontend frameworks and libraries such as React, Redux, TypeScript, Next.js, HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript (ES6+). Possessing a strong command of performance optimization techniques, including lazy loading, code splitting, and performance profiling, ensures optimal web vitals and user experience. Proficient in testing and quality assurance practices, utilizing Jest, Cypress, and various testing methodologies. Embraces Agile and CI/CD pipelines for efficient development workflows. Experienced in architecting micro-frontends and designing component-driven systems.

Key achievements include leading the development of real-time trading dashboards, integrating APIs for banking platforms, and implementing micro-frontend architecture for enhanced scalability and maintainability.

Passionate about building user-centric and accessible web applications, staying at the forefront of front-end technologies, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to drive innovation and achieve business goals. Open to new opportunities and challenging projects in the fintech space.
