About

I am Vijay Kumar Virigineni, a frontend developer with over four years of experience specializing in React.js and modern web architectures. I design and implement scalable, maintainable user interfaces that align user needs with business objectives.

At Plunk Foundation, I led the migration of the public website to a headless architecture using Next.js and Strapi. I developed a library of WCAG 2.1 AA–compliant React components and integrated React Hook Form for dynamic form management—resulting in a 25 % increase in volunteer sign-ups and newsletter conversions, and reducing content-publishing turnaround from several days to minutes.

During my tenure at EPAM Systems, I contributed to a knowledge-sharing platform serving over 10 000 users. I implemented interactive D3.js dashboards, standardized global state management with Redux, and introduced TypeScript alongside a test-driven development workflow. These efforts boosted test coverage to above 95 % and cut post-release defects by 80 %.

My core strengths include:

React & Next.js: component architecture, server-side rendering, performance tuning

TypeScript & Testing: static typing, Jest, React Testing Library, Cypress

Architectural Design: RESTful & GraphQL API integration, micro-frontends, AWS services (EC2, S3, Lambda)

Accessibility & Performance: semantic HTML, ARIA best practices, code-splitting, Core Web Vitals optimization

I thrive in cross-functional teams, translating product requirements into robust technical solutions and mentoring peers on React best practices. Outside of work, I build side projects like Nova Teach Dev (AI-generated homework) and Swift Spot Pay (geo-parking app) to explore emerging technologies.

I am now seeking a product-driven environment where I can apply my expertise in React, TypeScript, and cloud-native design to deliver measurable value. If you need a frontend developer who balances quality with efficiency, let’s connect.
