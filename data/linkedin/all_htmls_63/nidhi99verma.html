Is this the workplace reality we should accept? Don't get me wrong, I enjoy corporate culture. But there is a dark side. For example, sometimes I see: - Stellar performance reviews that never translate to compensation - "Critical team members" who only become critical once they resign - Companies that find raises impossible until replacement costs appear - Exit interviews that uncover solutions that could have prevented the exit So if you're navigating corporate life, and you're feeling undervalued... Remember, your worth doesn't depend on your company's willingness to acknowledge it before you threaten to leave. A company that only recognizes your value when you're halfway out the door isn't one that truly values you—it's one that values not having to replace you. True value recognition happens proactively, not reactively. Take ownership of your career by: - Communicating your achievements regularly - Setting clear expectations for growth - Testing your market value periodically - Being willing to walk away when necessary The truth is: Your best performance will be discussed when you want to resign, not when you want a salary raise.
