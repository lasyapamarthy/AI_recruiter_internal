Esteemed industry leaders shared their perspectives as both engineers and women in the energy sector. The discussion was enlightening, emphasizing that DEI is not about compromising qualifications but rather about fostering an inclusive environment that leverages diverse talents to drive innovation and growth in the industry. One of the highlights of my day was the opportunity to engage with representatives from various companies at their exhibition booths. The interactions were both insightful and inspiring. I had the pleasure of speaking with teams from Batoil Services Nigeria Limited, Batoil Services Nigeria Limited, Flosmart Energy Services LTD Lonadek Inc. , NDEL , Topline Limited , CECOM Technologies Limited, Oilfield Solutions Ltd., and KSB Company among others. The final panel session, expertly moderated by Riverson Oppong, PhD. delved into the themes of entrepreneurship and volunteering. Speakers including Olawale Ajayi, Ama Ikuru and my dear friend, Vice President of SPE, Unilag Chapter, in person of Samuel Uko provided valuable insights on the importance of volunteering in building professional networks and the delicate balance required to navigate entrepreneurship while pursuing a career in engineering. Their experiences were both motivating and instructive. This event reaffirmed my passion for the energy industry and reminded me of the core reasons I chose to pursue a career in petroleum and gas engineering. I am filled with anticipation for the future and eager to contribute meaningfully to the energy sector.
