We’ve seen this before, smartphones, messaging apps, etc. Once users grow accustomed to AI agents that just work natively on their devices, they’ll expect the same in their work environments. Enterprises won’t just adopt local AI agents, they’ll demand them: • 🔒 For data security (keeping IP on device), • ⚡ For speed and responsiveness (low latency processing), • 🧩 And for custom integration with proprietary tools and systems. MCP is the connective tissue that makes this possible: it provides a standardized way for AI agents to interact with the OS, securely and contextually. This isn’t just a new API.
