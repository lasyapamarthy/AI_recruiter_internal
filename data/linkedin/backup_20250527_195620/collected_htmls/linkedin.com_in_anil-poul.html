About

I am a passionate backend developer with a strong foundation in Java programming, Spring Framework, and modern development practices. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering from Dr. D. Y. Patil Institute of Technology, Pune, and a Diploma in the same stream from Government Polytechnic, Aurangabad. With a keen interest in solving complex problems, I specialize in building scalable and efficient backend systems.

My technical expertise spans Java (Core & Advanced), Data Structures, Spring Boot, REST APIs, Microservices, Hibernate, and SQL. I am also proficient in tools like Maven, Git, Selenium, TestNG, JUnit, and have hands-on experience with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, while currently learning Angular. My project portfolio includes a YouTube Clone, a Blog Application Backend using Spring Boot and MySQL, and a Beam Prediction in mmWave Communication research project.

I am certified in DSA with Java (Apna College) and Java Spring Framework 6 with Spring Boot 3 (Telusko, Udemy). Driven by curiosity and a growth mindset, I am excited to take on new challenges and contribute to impactful projects. Let’s connect and collaborate to bring innovative ideas to life!
