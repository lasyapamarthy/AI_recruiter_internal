Celebrating a New Certification Samuel’s profile photo Samuel Adewale reposted this View Mustapha Ahmad’s graphic link Mustapha AhmadMustapha Ahmad • 3rd+3rd+ Backend Developer (Python and sometimes Golang)Backend Developer (Python and sometimes Golang) 10mo • Edited • 10 months ago • Edited • Visible to anyone on or off LinkedIn Here is the motivation and demo of my project that won the University of Ilorin 2024 Best Student Innovation Award: LAILA: The Virtual Assistant for The Special Need Students. I gained admission to the University of Ilorin in 2018 to study Electrical and Electronic Engineering. A common practice in the Faculty of Engineering is taking courses with students from other faculties. In one such class, I noticed a man on the podium with the lecturer, making hand gestures. I was distracted because I wasn't familiar with what he was doing. After the class, I learned that he was a sign language translator. This made me realize how fortunate I was to follow lectures in real time without any impairment. However, I observed that the translator was often late or sometimes didn't show up at all, which made me think of ways to assist hearing-impaired students. In 2020, during the height of the COVID-19 pandemic, I began learning programming. I was also an avid fan of Marvel movies, particularly the interactions between Iron Man and Jarvis. Inspired by their dynamic, I decided to create something similar. Initially, I set out to build LAILA, which could take voice commands and execute tasks based on them. As I developed LAILA, I realized it could be adapted to display voice commands on a screen, thus assisting hearing-impaired students like those I had encountered at the university. However, I faced a significant challenge: LAILA relied heavily on internet connectivity, which is often unreliable in our country. Determined to find a solution, I along side Joel Ojerinde , Adenike Ajibade and Stephen Olaoke discovered the Vosk model, a machine learning model capable of performing real-time offline transcription. I integrated Vosk into LAILA, making it more reliable. I also recognized that hearing-impaired students weren't the only ones with special needs. Visually impaired students could benefit from LAILA as well. For them, LAILA can read specified books aloud. Thus, LAILA evolved to support different features for students with various special needs, enhancing their academic experience.
