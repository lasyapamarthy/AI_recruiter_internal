With the increasing popularity of IoT, incorporating IoT-based solutions can help in detecting bike crashes and notifying concerned authorities promptly. The proposed project aims to develop a bike crash detection system using IoT technology. • The bike crash detection system will be designed to detect the occurrence of an accident through a range of sensors integrated with the bike. The sensors will collect real-time data related to the bike's speed, acceleration, tilt, and position, and analyse it to identify whether an accident has occurred.
