While the outcome wasn’t what I hoped for, I’m proud of how much I learned and grew through this experience. This isn’t the end of the road—it’s just one step in my journey. I’m resetting, reworking, and preparing for my next opportunity. To anyone facing rejections: don’t lose hope.
