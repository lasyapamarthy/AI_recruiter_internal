Our team, comprising Mohit Jangra, Mudit Arya, Mohammad Danish, and Lakshyadeep Gogoi, delved into Problem Statement No. 1: Developing a Police Feedback System. This challenge aimed to enhance transparency and efficiency within law enforcement by creating a real-time platform for community members to provide feedback on police interactions. Here's a glimpse into our approach: 🌟 Rating Based Feedback Section: We designed a user-friendly dropdown menu allowing users to assign ratings out of 5 on various aspects of their interactions with the police. Admin customization enables adaptation to specific needs. 📝 Detailed Feedback Section: Beyond ratings, users can offer detailed feedback, providing invaluable insights for continuous improvement. 🤝 Community Section: Our platform functions akin to a social media platform, fostering interaction between citizens and the police. It serves as an open space for expressing concerns, sharing updates on regulations, and building awareness. 💬 Chatbot Assistant: To enhance accessibility, we incorporated a chatbot assistant to address common queries, ensuring a seamless user experience. And Many Features . Transparency is key to fostering trust between law enforcement and the community, and our solution embodies this ethos. Participating in the Hackathon provided us with the opportunity to engage with government officials and industry leaders, further amplifying the impact of our work.
