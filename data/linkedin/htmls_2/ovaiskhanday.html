ASER 2024 paints a worrying picture for education in Jammu & Kashmir. But who is responsible? We all are. The root cause: - Flawed Teacher Recruitment, Hiring is based on degrees, not teaching ability. A high percentage on paper doesn’t guarantee an engaging, effective educator. - Outdated Curriculum, If learning is boring and irrelevant, children won’t engage. Where is the grade-level curriculum that excites and inspires? - Lack of Technology Integration, We are in a digital world, but our classrooms are stuck in the past. Technology is either missing or misused, leaving students disconnected from modern learning. It’s time for ALL stakeholders, schools, policymakers, educators, and parents, to acknowledge this crisis and unlearn outdated practices. ✅ Redefine teacher recruitment, focus on teaching skills, not just degrees. ✅ Make curriculum student-friendly, relevant, and enjoyable. ✅ Integrate technology into learning, not just administration. ✅ Prioritize real learning over rote memorization.
