The interview started with basic technical questions, which the candidate answered well. Then, the team lead gave him a coding task: 👉 "Write a function that removes duplicate values from a nested array while preserving its structure (PHP)." The candidate struggled and couldn’t solve it. Rather than memorizing solutions, they adapt, learn, and implement on the spot using the tools and resources available to them. 💡 Instead of testing how well candidates remember syntax, we should focus on how effectively they apply problem-solving skills to practical challenges. let me know your take on this?
