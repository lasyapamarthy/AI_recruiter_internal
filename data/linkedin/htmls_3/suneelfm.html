Always repeat the question back to the interviewer. This simple step prevents your brain from jumping to conclusions and ensures you fully understand the problem. E is for Examples. Ask for specific input and output examples. Many candidates skip this important step. But verbalizing these examples can uncover hidden requirements. A means Approach. Always test your code against the examples you discussed earlier. This shows attention to detail and can catch errors you might have missed.
