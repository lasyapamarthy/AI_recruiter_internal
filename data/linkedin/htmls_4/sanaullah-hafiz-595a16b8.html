They’re seen as outdated, bloated, and ready for the trash bin. But here’s the truth: • They’ve handled real users, traffic, and edge cases. • They’ve survived years of business changes and still deliver value. • They’ve been battle-tested in ways shiny new systems haven’t. My take: 1- “Rewrite” is often a seductive trap. You want clean slates, but that’s rarely what the business needs. Don’t underestimate a system just because it’s old. -> Stability is a feature. One week in the legacy codebase > three months of onboarding docs.
