It’s because you don’t fully understand how JavaScript handles tasks behind the scenes. Concepts like the Call Stack, Microtask Queue, and Macrotask Queue often confuse developers, but they’re critical for answering tough interview questions. Bollywood Style: JavaScript Execution as a Family Drama Imagine ek Bollywood family drama: 1️⃣ Call Stack Hero ghar ka eldest son hai jo sabse pehle problem solve karta hai. Ek time pe ek kaam karne ka promise rakhta hai. 🕶️ Socho Shah Rukh Khan Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham mein, "Chhota mota kaam main hi sambhalta hoon!" Sirf synchronous tasks ka dhyan rakhta hai, aur agar hero busy hai, toh baki family (tasks) ko wait karna padta hai. ->The Call Stack is like the eldest son who handles one task at a time in the exact order they are received. It manages only synchronous tasks and will block other tasks until it is free. 2️⃣ Microtask Queue Hero ka younger sibling jo hamesha bolta hai, "Bhai, yeh kaam abhi urgent hai!" Yeh Promises resolve karta hai aur critical kaam turant karne ki priority rakhta hai. 🎩 Socho Varun Dhawan Humpty Sharma Ki Dulhania mein, "Main hero ke kaam ko turant smooth kar dunga." Microtasks, jaise Promises, tabhi stage lete hain jab hero ka busy schedule khatam ho. The Microtask Queue contains tasks like Promises and runs them as soon as the Call Stack is free. These tasks have higher priority over Macrotasks. 3️⃣ Macrotask Queue Chacha-ji jo bade event, jaise shaadi ke functions (setTimeout, setInterval), ke liye responsible hai. Chacha hamesha "Aap log free ho gaye ho? Accha, ab main shuru karoon?" ke mood mein rehta hai. 🤡 Imagine Paresh Rawal Hera Pheri mein, jo har kaam ke baad comedy karne aata hai. The Macrotask Queue handles larger, less urgent tasks like setTimeout, setInterval, or I/O events. These are executed only after the Call Stack and Microtask Queue are clear. Movie ka Flow (yaani JavaScript Execution): 1️⃣ Hero (Call Stack) ghar ki chhoti-moti ladai (synchronous code) ko sabse pehle solve karta hai. 2️⃣ Younger sibling (Microtask Queue) hamesha jaldi kaam niptata hai (Promises ko resolve karta hai). 3️⃣ Jab ghar ka kaam khatam ho jaye, tab Chacha-ji (Macrotask Queue) apna bada event organize karte hain. 1️⃣ The Call Stack handles all synchronous tasks first. 2️⃣ The Microtask Queue resolves high-priority tasks like Promises. 3️⃣ Finally, the Macrotask Queue executes larger jobs like setTimeouts or others Why Microtasks execute before Macrotasks: Microtasks have a higher priority as they are critical, like resolving Promises. How delays in the Call Stack affect asynchronous tasks: If the Call Stack is busy, Microtasks and Macrotasks must wait.
