I built a web app to coordinate an esports league for Boys & Girls Clubs across the nation, making it easier for them to organize matches and play together. Esports is the #1 thing kids ask for, and Boys & Girls Clubs are eager to offer programs. However, due to challenges like staff turnover and communication barriers, it's often hard for Clubs to get started. After talking with Clubs that wanted to compete, I organized the first national esports league for Super Smash Bros. Ultimate. To create the 6-week schedule, I collected team availability and wrote a Python script to find matchups. Kids had an amazing time, with some Clubs saying their game days felt like the Super Bowl. Seeing how much everyone loved it, I wanted to make the process smoother for the next season. I started building a web app with Next.js, tRPC, Chakra-UI, and Prisma. I added Discord authentication with Next-Auth since Discord was our main communication tool during matches. The app lets Clubs create accounts, manage teams, submit availability, view schedules, report results, and track team stats—all in one place. Scheduling was the hardest part. Clubs are in different time zones with varying availability, so it’s impossible to avoid some repeated matchups. I used a genetic algorithm to generate "high-quality" schedules, prioritizing unique pairings and balanced participation. Designing the mutation and crossover logic was tricky—I spent a lot of time figuring out what those should look like for schedules. I also tried constraint programming and integer linear programming but had trouble with the flexibility required. For rankings, I used win ratios and the Bradley-Terry algorithm. The app has been a game-changer. It’s saved me tons of time organizing leagues, and we’ve run two seasons with great feedback from Clubs. While no-shows still happen due to last-minute schedule changes, I’m working on a Discord bot for drop-in matches to address this. Overall, the app has made running esports leagues much easier and more fun for everyone involved. As always, if you’re curious about how it works (or know someone hiring web developers 😄), feel free to reach out! Here's the code: https://lnkd.in/gGtp-wBr Thanks for reading!
