I then started hopelessly searching for school-hour or flexible jobs that would work for my family. This time, as a mother, who feels proud of the new skills I have gained during this time raising my children. I have also joined as a woman who wants to raise awareness for the other women who are affected by taking time out of their career by having children. For those who may have been forced out of their previous job and now lack confidence in getting a new one. And for those struggling to return to work, or even apply for jobs, because they cannot find the flexibility they need for their family. So this time, I am not only proudly adding 'Full Time Parent' to my profile, alongside a new picture with some of my greatest work, but I am also adding my new business venture, the Five Hour Club: a community to help women return to work after children. Not only do I want to raise women up, and recognise the hard work that goes into building a family, but I also want to work closely with employers who also value their previous experience and new skills, so that when they are ready to return to work, there are flexible jobs available that suit their family needs. Together I believe that we can shape the future of flexible working, because let’s face it, working 9-5 doesn’t work for parents.
