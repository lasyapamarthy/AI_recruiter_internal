About

As a Software Developer, I thrive on crafting innovative solutions and tackling complex challenges from end to end. With expertise spanning React, React Native, Flutter, Node.js, and AWS (Lambda, API Gateway, Amplify, CloudWatch, Cognito, S3, DynamoDB), I bring ideas to life—whether it's building apps from scratch, optimizing user engagement, or integrating cutting-edge technologies like AI, blockchain (Polygon, Algorand), and NFC/RFID systems.

Key achievements:

1. Designed and developed an AI-powered app that generates text, images, and videos, managing everything from interface design to backend architecture using tools like React, Node.js, and MongoDB.
2. Delivered all assigned projects in 50% of the allocated time frame while maintaining high standards.
3. Independently built a Marketing website from scratch, handling full-stack development and designing scalable architecture on AWS.
4. Increased user engagement by 24% in just a month through optimization and close collaboration with clients.
5. Successfully integrated Vision AI-based QR codes and NFC/RFID technology for enhanced security and authenticity.
6. Led end-to-end production of Android and iOS applications, ensuring smooth deployments and seamless user experiences.
7. Mentored and supervised interns, fostering a culture of collaboration and growth.

I’m passionate about creating robust, scalable, and user-centric solutions that leave a lasting impact. Whether it’s working on AI-driven projects, blockchain innovations, or cloud architectures, I’m always eager to learn, grow, and contribute.

Let’s connect and explore how we can build something exceptional together!
