About

I am a results-driven software engineer and researcher with 7+ years of experience architecting secure, scalable cloud solutions and full-stack applications across diverse technologies. My expertise spans cloud infrastructure, security, and governance, with hands-on experience in AWS, GCP, Azure, Kubernetes, and Docker, enabling organizations to build resilient, high-performance systems.

At Samsung R&D, I led global identity and access management (IAM) initiatives, securing 1,000+ servers and databases, while streamlining compliance and governance. I also spearheaded the automation of enterprise email management, slashing response times and optimizing workflows.

Beyond cloud security, I have built and optimized full-stack applications across Java, Go, Python, Node.js, JavaScript (React, Angular, Next.js), and serverless architectures (Lambda, Firebase, GraphQL, REST, Kafka).

My recent work involves reimagining web application design by prioritizing RBAC policies, data privacy, and efficient access control. Instead of bulky frontends and backends, I focus on streamlined architectures that securely deliver data to the right users while reducing workflow processing times by 60% and simplifying 80% of application development

Currently pursuing an MS in Computer Science (Data Science specialization) at Stony Brook University, I am expanding my knowledge in Quantum Computing, Machine Learning, and Distributed systems. I thrive at the intersection of software engineering, cloud security, and AI, solving complex problems with an impact-driven approach.

Let’s connect to discuss cloud infrastructure, security, and the future of intelligent systems!

    Elevating Local Economies: A Novel Approach to Quantifying Small Business Significance

    Our groundbreaking project offers a fresh perspective on the pivotal role of small businesses within local communities. By harnessing a rich dataset encompassing government economic census and reviews from platforms like Yelp and Google Local, we've developed a robust success score for small businesses. This score considers various factors, including transit accessibility, pedestrian-friendliness, and the socio-economic backdrop of the area served. Our findings underscore the profound impact of small businesses on enhancing the sense of place and community well-being, providing invaluable insights for policymakers, urban planners, and entrepreneurs alike.

    ## Highlights:
    ‐ Evaluated significance of a business in terms of novel parameters obtained from features evaluating the economic, customer, social, and infrastructure impact of a business.
    ‐ Successfully validated ranking of businesses on these factors in community districts of Queens.
