    This project aims to compare the performance and accuracy of three popular short-read genome assembly is algorithms: Velvet, SPAdes, and ABySS. We will implement these algorithms on both simulated and real bacterial genomic datasets to evaluate their efficiency in terms of assembly quality, computational resource usage, and scalability. By benchmarking these methods against each other, we hope to provide insights into their strengths and weaknesses, guiding researchers in choosing the most appropriate tool for their specific genome assembly tasks. Our analysis will focus on key metrics such as N50 score, genome completeness, and misassembly rates.

    E-commerce Revolution: Investigate how internet growth has revolutionized e-commerce sales and its contribution to total retail volumes.

    IPO Insights: Analyze first-day returns of internet company IPOs to understand market dynamics and investor sentiment.

    Business Growth: Study the emergence and growth of new internet companies facilitated by internet usage expansion over time.

    TrendLens is accessible via a user-friendly web application, ensuring ease of use for all levels of expertise. Its intuitive interface and graphical visualizations empower users to customize analyses effortlessly and derive unique insights. Behind the scenes, powerful database processing supports complex computations to deliver robust analytical capabilities.

    Join us in unraveling the story of how the internet has reshaped our world with TrendLens, where data meets discovery at your fingertips.

Admin can add multiple distributors as well as that distributor can later add pharmacies accordingly. Admin can add individual medicines for chemist to order.

    Admin has all rights in which it can create new user and give number of or full rights to that user, it can add information regarding medicine i.e. its symptoms, side effects and other important information regarding medicine.

    Chemist have been given a login page from where they can be authenticated and later can order medicine. We have also integrated Payment system via Razor pay in which all types transfer possible i.e. via UPI ID, Credit/Debit card etc. Chemist can also see orders and generate bills from the system.

    The distributor has also its login page for authentication and to see pending orders. In this system we have made a special model in that once the chemist gives order the distributor has to accept it first depending on the stocks in inventory management. After accepting a bill is generated with amount for reference with amount and date. The most important feature we have used is that we have used machine learning Algorithm for Prediction of stocks of medicine depending upon the past orders and that too individually with specific medicine.
